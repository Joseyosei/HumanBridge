# Safety, privacy and responsible AI

Prototype only: no monitoring, diagnosis, treatment or live AI. Never collect real participant information in the prototype. Public repo: never commit secrets, private research data, logs or identifying records. Rotate credentials immediately if exposed. Do not paste access tokens into chat.

Before pilot: complete DPIA and determine UK GDPR lawful basis and any special-category condition; document minimisation, retention, deletion, processors and transfers. Seek ethics and safeguarding review. Establish incident response and location-appropriate support pathways. Consent is not a substitute for security. Do not promise emergency monitoring. AI must not encourage dependence, secrecy or isolation; evaluate with adversarial and demographic test cases. No user-to-user messaging until moderation, reporting and abuse prevention are operational. Do not infer clinical conditions from check-ins. Use only synthetic data for development. Production launch requires explicit safety and privacy signoff.

UK immediate danger: 999. Samaritans: 116 123, https://www.samaritans.org/.
