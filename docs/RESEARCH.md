# Research and evaluation

## Background
Loneliness is subjective; social isolation concerns objective social contact. Measure both separately. WHO's 2025 report establishes the public health importance of social connection; it does not establish effectiveness of this app.

## Research question
Does a personalised action plan improve loneliness and social participation compared with a non-personalised plan among adults aged 18–40?

## Proposed evaluation
Recruit an ethics-approved adult pilot, initially university students. Randomise to personalised vs generic interventions where feasible. Collect baseline and four-week follow-up, with a preregistered primary outcome using the ONS-recommended three-item loneliness measure. Record self-reported meaningful contact frequency as a separate outcome. Report effect sizes, uncertainty, missing data, safety events and attrition. Determine sample size before recruitment. Do not claim causality from an uncontrolled pilot. Research consent must be separate from app access. No collection before ethics and privacy approvals.

## Initial evidence sources
- WHO (2025), From loneliness to social connection: https://www.who.int/groups/commission-on-social-connection/report/
- ONS (2025), Measuring loneliness: https://www.ons.gov.uk/peoplepopulationandcommunity/wellbeing/methodologies/measuringlonelinessguidanceforuseofthenationalindicatorsonsurveys
- Hughes et al. (2004), A Short Scale for Measuring Loneliness in Large Surveys: https://doi.org/10.1177/0164027504268574

## Competitor review scope
Compare Meetup (events), BFF (friendship), Timeleft (group meetings), Wysa (wellbeing) and Replika (AI companionship). Validate current features, safety policies and pricing before formal competitive claims. This is a research protocol and preliminary evidence brief, not a completed systematic review.
